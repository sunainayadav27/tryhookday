Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Hookday ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you use our website and our content-planning service (the "Service"). Hookday is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, company name and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Brand data you give us: your brand's website address, products, target audience, regions, languages, tone, competitors and topics to avoid.
  • Connected social accounts (optional): if you connect a YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn business account, we receive the data you authorise, such as account name and ID, your public posts and their performance metrics, and hashtag search results. We never receive your social media passwords.
  • Usage data: which features you use, ideas you approve, edit or reject, device and browser type, and IP address, used to run and improve the Service.
  • Payment data: payments are handled by our payment provider (such as Razorpay). We receive only confirmation, plan and billing details, not your full card or bank details.

2. Public data we analyse

To suggest content, we analyse publicly available information such as trending videos, search trends, news articles, government announcements and festival calendars, using official APIs and public feeds. We use it to detect topics, not to build profiles of individuals, and we do not store the personal details of people who appear in public posts beyond what is needed to show a link to the source.

3. How we use your data

  • To create your account and provide the Service, including your content calendar and suggestions.
  • To measure which suggestions work and improve them for your brand.
  • To send service emails (such as your weekly plan and alerts you turn on) and, with your consent, product updates.
  • To process payments, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.

We rely on your consent and on the legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing done before withdrawal.

4. AI processing

We use third-party AI model providers to analyse topics and draft content ideas. We send them only the information needed for each request, under agreements that do not allow them to use your data to train their models.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Service (cloud hosting, AI model providers, email, analytics and payments), under contracts that require them to protect it, or where the law requires.

6. Data from Google and YouTube

Hookday uses YouTube API Services. By using features that rely on YouTube data you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Hookday's access to your Google data at any time from your Google security settings.

7. Storage and security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers, preferably in India. We use encryption in transit, access controls and regular backups. Data from connected social accounts is refreshed or deleted in line with each platform's rules.

8. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep records (such as invoices) by law.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. To delete your data, follow our data deletion instructions. Email us for any other request; we reply within 30 days.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.
