Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 23 November 2026

You can ask Hookday to delete your data at any time. This includes data we received from Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn or any other connected account.

Option 1: Delete your account in Hookday

  1. Log in to Hookday.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account, brands, calendars and connected-account data are deleted within 30 days.

Option 2: Email us

Email privacy@tryhookday.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

Removing access from a connected platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations on Instagram), select Hookday and click Remove. When you remove Hookday this way, we delete the data we received from Meta for your account.
  • YouTube / Google: visit Google account permissions and remove Hookday.
  • LinkedIn: go to Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services, and remove Hookday.

What we keep

We may keep invoices and payment records where Indian tax law requires, and anonymised statistics that cannot identify you or your brand.
